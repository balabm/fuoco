Our Craft

Three things we never compromise

Everything on the menu — all nine chapters of it — is built on the same three foundations.

01

Fire

Our wood-fired oven runs hot all service — blistered crusts, charred edges, and that unmistakable ember-kissed flavor.

02

Flour

Dough stretched by hand, pasta sheets rolled in-house daily, focaccia baked for every basket of soup and salad.

03

The Sea

The day's catch goes straight from the boats into our seafood spaghetti, soups and grilled plates.

Wecookwithfire.Everythingelsefollows.

A wood-fired oven, organic basil, the day's catch — and a room that glows like the coals themselves.
